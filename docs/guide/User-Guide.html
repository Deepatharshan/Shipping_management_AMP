Make a commercial invoice

  1. Click Commercial Invoice (or + at the top right for a new one).
  2. Step 1 — Proforma invoice: type the PI number (e.g. 61001/M/S) and press Enter or Fetch details. The consignee, port of discharge and every item come in. Or click Browse proformas and pick one.
  3. Step 2: type the commercial invoice no (e.g. M/26/021) and check the date, consignee and ASN no. If the number is already in the FoxPro table (made by the old program), click Use FoxPro record.
  4. Step 3 — Shipment: vessel, voyage, ETD, ETA, container no, container seal no, payment terms.
  5. Goods & order: PO no, POD, incoterms — printed above the items.
  6. Items shipped: change quantities to what was actually shipped; the total is qty × price (a total you type over is kept and outlined). Add barcodes once — they are filled in automatically on the next invoice for the same items.
  7. Totals, marks & reference, Certificate, Beneficiary & bank: type or change the text; leave "Say" blank for the automatic amount in words.
  8. Preview to check, then Save. Save PDF and Print are in the dark bar at the bottom.
When a value is longer than its FoxPro field, a note under the field shows what FoxPro will keep (e.g. payment terms T/T 30 DAYS → T/T). The full text is still printed and kept in the app.

Find, reprint, delete

  • Shipments: every invoice in the app. Double-click to open; the printer button prints; the bin deletes (tick "also mark the FoxPro record deleted" if needed — it can be recalled).
  • Proforma Invoices: the proformas in the Product Management App → Make commercial invoice.
  • FoxPro Records: every record in the old shipping table. Click one to see every field, edit it, delete or recall it, or Open as invoice.
  • Change Log: what was saved or deleted, when, and the old and new FoxPro values.

An index file does not match the tableAsk the admin: Settings → Rebuild ticked indexes (with FoxPro closed).
Do not keep the shipping table open for writing in FoxPro while this app saves. The app copies the table and its index before its first write each day.

For the admin

  • First logins: ampshipping / Ship@2026; Settings password Admin@2026. Change them in Settings → Logins.
  • Settings → FoxPro shipping table: choose deldem shipping.DBF; SHIPPING.IDX next to it is ticked automatically. Switch on Save to the FoxPro shipping table when ready.
  • Settings → Product Management App database: on the product manager's PC share %APPDATA%\Photo Quotation Generator\quotations, then choose \\PC-NAME\quotations\quotations.db here.
  • Settings → Backup: choose a folder on another drive and click Back up now regularly.
